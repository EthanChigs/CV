Understanding qualification levels

Levels, results, and supporting documents.

An NQF level describes the complexity of study; it is separate from a result or grade. University module levels are shown individually. For foreign qualifications, South African comparability depends on a formal evaluation rather than the title alone. SAQA explains the evaluation process.
