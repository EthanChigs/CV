Debate Club

Secretary from 2023 to 2024. I helped run weekly practice debates, coached the junior team, and represented the club at inter-school nationals-style tournaments — plus one memorable final on the motion “schools should ban smartphones.”

School Choir

Tenor for six years. We performed at the district and provincial music festivals each season, and I was section leader in 2021 — which mostly meant keeping twelve tenors on the same note.

Robotics & Science Club

Joined in 2023. My team built a line-following robot for the university STEM fair and took second place. I look after the software — the robot’s terrible dance moves are, however, a team effort.
